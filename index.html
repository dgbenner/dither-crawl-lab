Dither Crawl Lab

Halftone shadows on live UI. Each shadow is one soft shape, and every pixel is a dot or not depending on a noise value that keeps changing. Pixels deep in the shadow always come out dark, pixels far away always come out empty, and the ones at the edge flip back and forth. That flipping is the crawl.

Settings

Real HTML underneath

The text field, tabs, switch and button are ordinary form elements with zero opacity. They take the clicks, typing, focus and screen reader. Turn on "Show the hidden HTML" to see them.

Custom renderer on top

A low-resolution canvas reads each element's position and state every frame (value, caret, checked, hover, focus) and redraws it in three colors, then scales up with hard pixel edges.

Shadow by threshold

Shadow strength at each pixel is compared to a noise value. Re-rolling part of the noise each update moves only the in-between pixels, so the core holds still and the fringe crawls.
